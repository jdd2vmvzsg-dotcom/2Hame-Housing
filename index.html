❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is 2Hame free to use?

Yes! Basic property listings are completely free. Premium features (featured listings, advanced analytics) are available at affordable rates.

How do I verify a property listing?

We verify owners and agents through official documentation (ID, KRA PIN, ownership documents). Verified listings receive a trust badge.

Can I list land for sale?

Absolutely. We support residential, commercial, agricultural, and investment land listings of all sizes.

Is 2Hame available outside Kenya?

Currently focused on Kenya, but we're expanding to other African countries in 2025.

How do I contact the property owner?

Each listing has a contact form, phone number, and WhatsApp link. Owners and agents respond directly.
